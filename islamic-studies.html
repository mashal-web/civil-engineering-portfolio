Islamic Studies
& Ethical Behavior.

Course information and lecture plan transcribed from the supplied SSUET course information sheet. The dates below belong to the Fall 2025 session and are retained as an archive, not presented as a current timetable.

01 / COURSE DETAILS

Purpose and learning outcomes

The course information sheet describes a focus on core Islamic beliefs and requirements, love, peace, human prosperity, and the five domains of creeds, worship, values, economics and ethics through the Quran and Hadith.

INSTITUTION

Sir Syed University of Engineering & Technology

Department of Software Engineering · Session Fall 2025

INSTRUCTOR

Asma Perveen

Teaching mode: synchronous. The sheet lists Monday office hours, 8:30 am–5:00 pm.

CLO 1 · C2

Core Islamic beliefs

Explain core Islamic beliefs and principles to address contemporary faith and life issues. Mapped to PLO-9 (Ethics).

CLO 2 · C3

Ethical application

Interpret Islamic teachings and apply ethical values from the Quran and Hadith to balance religious and social responsibilities. Mapped to PLO-9 (Ethics).

02 / COURSE OUTLINE

Subject areas

01

Faith and worship

Articles of Faith, Pillars of Islam and the Holy Quran.

02

Quran and Hadith

Quranic verses, Hadith, compilation, types and selected translations.

03

Prophetic life

Life of the Holy Prophet, lessons from Makki and Madani life, and human rights.

04

Law and jurisprudence

Primary and secondary sources, Ijma, Qiyas, Fiqh and schools of thought.

05

Science and governance

Islam and science, Muslim scientists, and the Islamic political system.

06

Economics and society

Islamic economic system, lawful earnings, prohibition of usury, culture, civilization, rights and duties.
