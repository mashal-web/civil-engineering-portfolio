01 / COURSE OVERVIEW

Purpose

The course aims to build foundational mathematics and statistics skills so students can interpret, analyze and apply quantitative information. It combines theoretical knowledge with practical exercises to develop quantitative literacy, problem-solving and critical thinking in the context of their field of study.

INSTITUTION

Sir Syed University of Engineering & Technology

INSTRUCTOR

Teaching mode: synchronous, asynchronous and hybrid/blended.

02 / TOPIC MAP

What the course covers

01

Number & measurement

Number systems, arithmetic operations, units and conversions, dimensions, area, perimeter and volume.

02

Ratios & data

Rates, ratios, proportions, percentages, data types, measurement scales, tables and graphs.

03

Geometry & algebra

Lines, angles, circles, polygons, sets, exponents, factoring, expressions, relations and functions.

04

Equations & inequalities

Algebraic and graphical solutions of linear and quadratic equations and inequalities.

05

Statistics

Population and sample, mean, median, mode, dispersion and data interpretation.

06

Counting & probability

Multiplicative counting, permutations, combinations, basic probability, random variables and probability distributions.
