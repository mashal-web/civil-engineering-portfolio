This site records my development as a civil engineering student and the technical ideas I explore along the way.

Why this portfolio exists

Civil engineering connects mathematics, materials, structures, infrastructure and the built environment. I use this portfolio to organize my learning, document practical exercises and communicate progress clearly.

The aim is not to present myself as an experienced professional. It is to create a reliable record of student work, questions, skills in development and lessons learned.

Areas of interest

  • Structural systems and load paths
  • Engineering drawing and technical communication
  • Construction materials and their properties
  • Digital tools for engineering workflows
  • Clear technical writing and evidence-based learning

How to read this site

Project pages distinguish conceptual exercises from verified results. Learning notes are educational summaries, not a substitute for engineering codes, instructor guidance or professional review.
